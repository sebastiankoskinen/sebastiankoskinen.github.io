The Concept

L∞RE is a secondhand store. They stock quality clothing from any brand — not necessarily expensive, just good pieces worth keeping alive. Before anything goes on the rack, every garment gets an NFC chip sewn into its seam and the ∞ mark on its body. The chip is washable. The chain is permanent. That's where it gets interesting.

Scan the mark. Find everyone who wore this before you — their fits, their stories, the nights it lived through. Add yours. Sell when you're ready. The archive travels with the garment to whoever comes next. The older the piece, the deeper the gallery.

How it works
  • I
    The mark Every piece leaves the store with an NFC chip in the seam and the ∞ mark on the body. That mark is the portal.
  • II
    Your chapter Scan the mark. Link a post from your Instagram or TikTok wearing the piece. That becomes your entry in the garment's living timeline.
  • III
    Wear it Wear it to the places it deserves. Add more posts whenever. Your chapter stays open as long as the garment is yours.
  • IV
    Pass it on Sell through L∞RE or anywhere else. The chip keeps the archive intact. The chain never breaks.
  • V
    The inheritance The next owner scans and finds everything. Including you. Owner 10 inherits nine lives.
